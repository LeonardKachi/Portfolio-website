About Me

I'm a Cloud Security Engineer & Architect who builds systems that don't just run - they defend, adapt, and scale. I merge DevSecOps principles, Zero Trust frameworks, and AI-driven security analytics to transform cloud infrastructures into resilient ecosystems.
